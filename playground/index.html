Was this machine purchase usable?

Archived research demo (published 2026-08-01). This synthetic APQV vocabulary is preserved as history. It is not the current Perpensum product contract or live integration. For CEN and the Decision Engine, see the current docs.

A provider can return succeeded while the buyer still receives an unusable result. Change the evidence below to see how Agentic Purchase Quality Verification separates the two.

APQV is a proposed category, not an adopted standard. It names the buyer-side process of comparing a purchase mandate with the seller promise, transaction, delivery, and observed outcome before the next machine purchase. Read the proposal.

This public demo uses synthetic data. Nothing is sent to a server or saved.

Why

    Evidence gaps

      Fraud conclusion

      Not determined. A mismatch or failure is not, by itself, proof of fraud.

      What this demonstrates

      • Purchase authorization and provider success do not establish buyer-side usability.
      • Pre-purchase criteria make the outcome comparison reproducible.
      • The result can change the next purchase action: allow, hold, retry, or collect evidence.

      What it does not claim

      • No real seller, product, or transaction is evaluated.
      • No absolute quality, satisfaction, ROI, or fraudulent intent is determined.
      • No cross-buyer benchmark or market price is used.
