What Perpensum is

A buyer-side Collective Experience Network and Decision Engine for machine purchases. It does not execute purchases, hold provider credentials, publish named-seller scores, or promise a recommendation when the evidence is thin.

Publisher and independence

Perpensum is published by PARADE and is being developed as an independent buyer-side service. We do not accept compensation from evaluated sellers in exchange for changing evidence, eligibility, or advice.

Historical research on Agent-Directed Manipulation, APQV, and the synthetic playground remains available in the public research repository, but it is not the current product contract.
