The product

Experience first. Decisions second.

A payment dashboard can show what one account spent. Perpensum preserves the context, alternatives, choice, and observed result needed to learn what happened—and makes only bounded use of comparable experience.

Ask before purchase

The buyer seals the need, complete action set, constraints, and Outcome plan before seeing the result.

Record what happened

The buyer purchases through its existing route, then appends the Choice and typed Outcome or explicit missing observation.

Answer within evidence

The Decision Engine separates direct evidence from looser signals and may prefer, collect more, or abstain. It does not invent certainty.

Why a network

A single buyer can optimize only from the failures and alternatives it has already seen. CEN is meant to preserve independent experience across buyers without turning raw customer data into a shared database.

Data boundary

The purchasing evidence enters. The private payload does not.

Stays with the buyer

  • provider and payment credentials
  • raw prompts, inputs, and outputs
  • customer records and private artifacts
  • purchase execution and final authority

Private purchase ledger

  • typed goal and situation
  • complete considered actions
  • choice and purchase evidence digest
  • typed Outcome, missingness, and time

Collective projection

  • only consented, eligible fields
  • independent-control accounting
  • support, uncertainty, and limitations
  • no public named-seller score
