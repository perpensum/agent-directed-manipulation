Who fits

An AI-native team already using Replicate for structured invoice extraction, already able to choose between two or more model offerings for that same job, and paying a real cost when a choice fails, arrives late, or requires rework.

The loop

Three calls around the purchase you already make

  1. Ask before purchase.
    Your AI seals the need, considered actions, constraints, and how the result will be observed. Perpensum returns bounded evidence or says that evidence is insufficient.
  2. Record the Choice.
    Your AI buys through its existing route. Perpensum does not hold provider credentials or execute the purchase.
  3. Report the Outcome.
    Your AI records a typed result or an explicit missing observation. The private history updates, and eligible fields may contribute to the Collective Experience Network.

What stays with you

  • provider and payment credentials
  • raw prompts, inputs, and outputs
  • customer records and private artifacts
  • the final purchasing decision and execution

What Perpensum stores

  • typed goal, situation, options, choice, and outcome
  • offering and release identity
  • evidence digests and observation times
  • missingness and retractions

No public seller score. Cross-buyer price sharing is off for this pilot.

What you receive

A decision service that knows when not to answer

Perpensum separates directly comparable experience from looser signals. It may return a bounded preference, ask for more evidence, or abstain. It does not claim that logged associations are causal, assign a universal quality score, or buy on your behalf.
